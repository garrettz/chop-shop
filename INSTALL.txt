Chop Shop — a slice sequencer Add On for LUNA
Built on @uaudio/luna-builder-sdk 3.1.2.63. Needs LUNA 3.1.1 or newer, on macOS.

Install
  1. Unzip this download. You get "Chop Shop.lunaaddon" (one file) and this note.
  2. Close any open session in LUNA first (save it), then open "Chop Shop.lunaaddon" with LUNA (double-click it,
     or drag it onto LUNA): LUNA installs it into its Add Ons folder.
     (Installing with a session open, LUNA swaps it for a new empty "Untitled" session, and an open Chop Shop window
     loses its tracks. If that happens: close the Chop Shop window, open it again, and pick your session from Recent.)
     If your LUNA doesn't take it: rename it to "Chop Shop.zip", unzip it, and copy the "Chop Shop" folder into
       /Library/Application Support/Universal Audio/LUNA/Add Ons/
     (create that folder if it isn't there; your user needs to be able to write to it).
  3. In LUNA, open a session, then the Add Ons menu → Chop Shop. No restart needed.
     LUNA 3.1.2 and newer ask once which servers Chop Shop may reach: Allow, or the example packs can't download
     their other songs (and sound generation and track naming can't reach their services). Change it later in
     LUNA's Settings → Add Ons → SERVERS.
  Updating: install the new one the same way, then close the Chop Shop window and open it again.

Start here
  "Take the tour" on the start screen: Glitchy shows you round the shop in a few minutes, sound on. The tour is a
  sandbox: it doesn't touch your LUNA session. Skip section and Skip Tutorial are at the top right; ✕ or Esc ends it.
  The ? key (or ⋯ → Guide) opens the Guide: every screen, with pictures.

Sounds
  Three example packs to play with: Main Stage (EDM), Night Drive (synthwave) and Velvet Hours (neo-soul and R&B).
  Main Stage is ready to go: its sounds (and the tour's) come down from the pack site (lowdownsound.io) as they're first
  played, once, then stay on this Mac, so the first Generate and the tour need the internet. Night Drive and Velvet
  Hours download free with one click on their cards. Open one on the start screen, pick how hard to chop (One shots, Chop, Experimental) and how many
  bars, and Generate: Chop Shop makes a fresh LUNA session and sketches a starting point from the pack. Each pack's
  songs come down from the pack site the first time you Generate with it (once; then they stay on this Mac). The other
  packs on the list are coming soon.
  Your own sounds:
    - ＋ Add your pack: turn a folder of samples (a sample pack, your own recordings) into a pack of your own.
    - Find samples: listen through any folder on your Mac, keep the good ones, or make a song from the ones you pick.
    - Resources: where to get more sounds, free and paid.
  Dropping files onto Chop Shop doesn't work inside LUNA yet; use Add your pack or Find samples.

Pads
  The pads key in Chop Shop's header opens its pads. Play them with the mouse, the computer keys (1–4, Q–R, A–F, Z–V)
  or a controller (LUNA passes it on, no other software needed).

Good to know
  - Chop Shop writes your pattern into LUNA as audio clips on "Chop" tracks. It needs an open session.
  - The first time a hit plays in LUNA, its audio uploads; give it a moment.
  - Effects are LUNA's own plugins, on each Chop track.
  - Optional, with your own keys (⋯ → Functions → API keys): generate sounds from a description (ElevenLabs, Stable
    Audio or fal.ai) and have Claude name your tracks (Anthropic). None ships in this package; keys stay on your Mac
    and go only to their provider, billed to your account there.
  - Something wrong? ⋯ → Functions → Download logs, and send the file along with what happened.
