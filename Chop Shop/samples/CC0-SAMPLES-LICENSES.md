# CC0 samples in Chop Shop's genre packs

The files listed below (`ChopShop_<pack>_…`, marked `"source": "cc0"` in `index.json`) are made from public-domain
recordings: CC0 1.0 Universal samples, cut into one-shot strips, or played note by note (resampled to pitch) into loops
in each pack's key and tempo. The vocal ad-libs and vocal chops (`ChopShop_<pack>_vocal_…`) were made for Chop Shop with
ElevenLabs on a paid plan, which allows commercial use; the vocoder voices are those chops through Chop Shop's own
vocoder. The sounds from Freesound (CC0) are credited in FREESOUND-CC0.md. Every other sound in this folder is
synthesized in code. CC0 asks for nothing; we credit the authors anyway.

Every file in `processed/` comes from one of the fourteen sources below (sections 6–14 added on 2026-11-07). Each one is CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain
dedication), as its author says on the author's own page and/or in the repository's `LICENSE` file. The quotes below
are copied verbatim from those texts. Copies of the texts, as fetched on 2026-11-04 (sections 1–5) and 2026-11-07 (6–14), are in `meta/`, and
`scripts/process.mjs` checks that every quote still appears in its saved copy before it writes `manifest.json`.

CC0 asks for nothing: no attribution, no notice, no share-alike. We credit the authors anyway (see "Credits" at the
end), because it costs nothing and two of the older readme files ask for it as a courtesy.

**Disclosed, not a license term:** the repository's 2016 `Readme.txt`
